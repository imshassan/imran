Published work

Publications

Two books published by Save the Children in 2015, illustrated entirely with my photographs from rural and urban Bangladesh. Open a book to turn its pages.

Save the Children · 2015

Extreme Poor Livelihoods Programming: Collection of Instruments

অতিদরিদ্র জীবিকায়ন কর্মসূচী: সহায়ক উপকরণ সংগ্রহ

A bilingual handbook of the tools Save the Children used to help nearly 45,000 extreme poor households in south-west Bangladesh build assets, skills and food security. My photographs follow the families and communities at the heart of the programme.

  • PublisherSave the Children
  • Year2015, Dhaka
  • Pages100
  • LanguageEnglish & Bangla
  • PhotographerS M Imran Hassan

All photographs © S M Imran Hassan / Save the Children

Save the Children · 2015

The Practicalities of Informed Consent in Development Photography

A short guide, based on research in Bangladesh in late 2014, on how photographers and development workers can take truly informed consent when photographing vulnerable people. Written by Nabila Idris and illustrated with my portraits.

  • Year2015, Dhaka
  • Pages16
